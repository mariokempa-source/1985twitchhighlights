Stream highlights, ready for TikTok.

1985 Twitch Highlights is a small creator tool that turns the best moments of German Twitch streams into subtitled, vertical highlight videos and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account. Every streamer featured has given us permission.

How it works

1. Find

Top clips of streamers who agreed to be featured are selected using the official Twitch API.

2. Edit

Clips are cropped to a 9:16 vertical format (1080×1920), and German subtitles are generated with speech recognition that runs on our own computer.

3. Publish

The finished video of 60 to 90 seconds is posted to the TikTok account of the app owner with the TikTok Content Posting API, with credit to the streamer.

For streamers

We only feature streamers who agreed beforehand. Every video names the streamer and links back to their Twitch channel. If you are featured and want a video taken down, or you want to stop being featured, write to us and we will remove it promptly.

What the app does with TikTok

  • It signs in the app owner's own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • It uploads the owner's finished videos through the Content Posting API.
  • It does not read other users' content, send messages, follow accounts, or collect data about other TikTok users.

Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
