Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Who we are

1985 Twitch Highlights ("the app", "we") is operated by Mario Kempa, Germany. Contact: mariokempa@googlemail.com. The app is a personal creator tool. It is used only by its operator, to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account.

2. Data we process

  • TikTok account data. When the operator signs in with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok gives the app an access token and the account's open ID. We request only the scopes needed to upload and publish videos (video.publish or video.upload) and to show which account is connected (user.info.basic).
  • Public Twitch data. Through the official Twitch API we read public information about clips of streamers who agreed to be featured: clip title, view count, duration, creation date and the clip itself.
  • Videos we create. Edited videos and automatically generated subtitles, made from those clips.

We do not collect personal data from visitors of this website. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools.

3. How we use the data

  • To sign in to TikTok and publish the operator's videos through the Content Posting API.
  • To select, edit and subtitle clips of consenting streamers.

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of individuals.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored only in a private configuration file on the operator's own computer and are never published. Videos and logs are stored on the same computer. Speech recognition for subtitles runs locally; audio is not sent to a third party.

5. Retention and deletion

The TikTok access token is kept only while the account is connected. The operator can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services, after which the token is deleted from the app. Local video files can be deleted at any time.

6. Third-party services

The app uses the TikTok API (see TikTok's Privacy Policy) and the Twitch API (see Twitch's Privacy Notice). Their own policies apply to data they process.

7. Your rights

If you are in the EU, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data and to object to its processing under the GDPR. Featured streamers can ask us at any time to remove a video that contains them. To exercise any right, email mariokempa@googlemail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
